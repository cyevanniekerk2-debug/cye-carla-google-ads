QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Google Ads management FAQs.

Send Prime Digital a WhatsApp message if you need help choosing a starting point.

Is the Google advertising budget included?

No. Prime Digital's package fee covers campaign setup, management and optimisation. The advertising budget is paid separately to Google.

Can you guarantee first position on Google?

No legitimate provider can guarantee a permanent number-one ad or organic ranking. Placement depends on Google's live auction, relevance, quality, competition, bidding and other factors.

What do you need from me to start?

We will ask about your business, services, service area, ideal customers, offer, website and preferred Google advertising budget.

Do you work across South Africa?

Yes. Campaigns can target South Africa nationally or focus on selected provinces, cities and service areas.
