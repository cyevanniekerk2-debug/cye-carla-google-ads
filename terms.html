Service Terms

These draft terms are a product-design placeholder and should be professionally reviewed before Prime Digital accepts live orders.

Orders & payment

EFT payments: where EFT is offered, the customer must use the Prime Digital order reference and provide the requested order details. Proof-of-payment documents assist reconciliation but do not by themselves establish cleared funds. An EFT order is activated only after Prime Digital verifies receipt in the nominated bank account.

Customers will review the selected service, full price, recurring charges where applicable, billing frequency and order information before final submission. An order is treated as paid only after the payment provider confirms successful payment.

Advertising

Google Ads results are not guaranteed. Clicks, calls, enquiries, cost per click and conversions vary by industry, competition, targeting, budget, website quality and auction conditions.

Managed websites

Managed websites remain hosted and maintained by Prime Digital while the selected managed plan is active. Client-supplied content remains the client's content. Domain registrant details should identify the client. A handover/buyout option may be available under the applicable package terms.

Domains & email

Domains are managed on behalf of customers and remain subject to registry/registrar rules. Email services are subject to infrastructure limits and acceptable-use requirements. Suspensions should follow the applicable notice and grace process described at checkout.

Recurring services

Recurring services will clearly state the billing frequency, renewal date, included services and cancellation method. Cancellation does not automatically transfer third-party licences or services unless expressly included.

Customer responsibilities

Customers must provide accurate information, lawful content and the permissions needed for materials they submit.
