DRAFT FOR FINAL LEGAL REVIEW

Privacy & POPIA Notice

This draft explains the intended handling of customer personal information by Prime Digital. It must be completed with the final company registration details, Information Officer details, processors, retention periods and contact email before launch.

Information we may collect

Account details, business information, contact information, billing records, service briefs, domain registrant information, support communications, website analytics and information needed to provide purchased services.

Why we process information

To create and manage accounts, fulfil services, process and reconcile payments, communicate about orders and renewals, provide support, prevent fraud, maintain security, comply with legal obligations and improve our services.

Your choices and rights

Customers will be provided mechanisms to access or correct account details, manage communication preferences and submit privacy requests. Certain records may need to be retained where law or legitimate operational requirements apply.

Security

Prime Digital intends to use access controls, secure authentication, encrypted transport, least-privilege administration, secure payment providers and appropriate backups. Payment-card data should be handled by the payment provider rather than stored directly by Prime Digital where possible.

Sharing

Information may be shared only with service providers required to deliver the selected service, such as payment processors, registrars, hosting/email infrastructure and advertising platforms, subject to appropriate contracts and safeguards.
