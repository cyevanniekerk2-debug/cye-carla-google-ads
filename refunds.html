Refunds, Cancellations & Renewals

The final policy must be reviewed against the exact service type and applicable South African consumer/e-commerce law before live checkout.

Customers will be able to review and correct order details before final payment.

Prime Digital intends to send renewal/payment notices and apply a reasonable grace process before suspending managed services. Exact notice periods will be finalised before launch.
